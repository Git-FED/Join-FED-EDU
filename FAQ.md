# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where can I find the main profile?

The main profile is [Git-FED](https://github.com/Git-FED). Use [fed-edu](https://github.com/fed-edu) for the organization onboarding flow.

## Is following the organization the same as joining it?

No. Following starts the onboarding process. You become a member only after accepting the invitation.

## Where is the Join button?

It is normally in the GitHub invitation email. You can also use the bell-shaped Notifications icon or the banner on the [fed-edu organization page](https://github.com/fed-edu).

## What if I used the wrong GitHub account?

Sign out, sign in to the invited account, and open the invitation again.

## How long is an invitation valid?

GitHub invitations commonly expire after seven days. Ask @git-fed to resend an expired invitation.

## Does joining the organization grant every repository?

No. Repository access is controlled by organization and team permissions.

## What should I include in a help request?

Your username, the step where you are stuck, the exact error, and whether you checked spam and Notifications. Never include passwords or tokens.

## Who sends the invitation?

The organization administrator, [@git-fed](https://github.com/git-fed), sends invitations.

## Where do I report a security issue?

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

## Can I suggest wording changes?

Yes. Open a pull request following [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Where are support and funding links?

See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) and [.github/FUNDING.yml](.github/FUNDING.yml).
